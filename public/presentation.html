02 · The New Standard of Networking
Paper vs. B-SMART™

Why hand someone a piece of paper when you can showcase your business?

Paper business cards were built for another era. With B-SMART™, your business is shared across platforms, accessed instantly, and experienced digitally — so every share creates an opportunity to engage customers.

Traditional Print

Paper Business Cards

Static ink on cardboard that stops working the moment your prospect walks away.

  • 88% of paper cards are lost or thrown away within a week
  • Expensive reprints whenever staff, numbers, or services change
  • No clickable WhatsApp, calendar booking, or service catalog
  • Manual phone typing creates friction and missed callbacks
  • Zero visibility into views, clicks, or customer interest
Interactive Platform

B-SMART™ Digital Business Card

An always-on business profile that actively turns introductions into customer inquiries.

  • Saved directly to Android & iOS contacts in a single tap
  • Update phone numbers, hours, banners, or pricing live anytime
  • Built-in WhatsApp chat, MS Teams / Outlook booking, and media galleries
  • Integrated callback inquiry form captures customer opportunities fast
  • Real-time tracking of card views, shares, calls, and vCard saves
